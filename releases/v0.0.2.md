## Highlights ⭐️

* **Ten themes**: Kaishi, Kinetic, Lucid, Prism, Quill, Stablo and Voxt join Apex, Atlas and Velocity. Prism is a financial-infrastructure marketing theme.
* **A showcase with downloads**: A generated showcase site presents every theme, with downloadable theme archives.
* **Richer pages**: Structured data, multilingual pages, responsive screenshots, crawler files and registry metadata.
* **Wider automated checks**: Accessibility, contrast, CSP, link, page-weight and Lighthouse coverage expanded, and hundreds of cross-theme accessibility and browser defects found by the broader audit were fixed.
