## Highlights ⭐️

* **Three complete themes**: Apex, Atlas and Velocity ship as full SSG themes, rebuilt around template inheritance, with registry manifests, documentation, screenshots and multilingual content.
* **One shared baseline**: The themes share light and dark mode behaviour, responsive layouts and content schemas.
* **Quality gates from the start**: Automated build, contrast, semantics, link and page-weight gates run on every change.
