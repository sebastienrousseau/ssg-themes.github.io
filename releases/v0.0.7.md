## Highlights ⭐️

* **A gallery you can filter and return to**: Category chips above the tiles show one kind of theme at a time, with counts taken from the tiles, and every theme demo carries an "All 26 themes" link back to the gallery, in English or French.
* **The site theme takes the site's layouts, and passes AAA**: The sebastienrousseau theme gets the column layouts and type scale of sebastienrousseau.com and stays inside a 320 px viewport. The AAA suite reached it for the first time and its fixes went to the site too: 44 px targets on standalone links, genre labels at AA contrast, and a Send button that stays visible in forced colours.
* **Accessible names and images the generator accepts**: Brand links, copy buttons and search triggers in 18 themes have an accessible name that contains their visible text (WCAG 2.5.3), and the jspassgen logo is an inline SVG that ssg 0.0.72's checks accept.
* **Gates that measure only this repository**: The browser suites no longer wait on third-party embeds, axe measures pages after their entrance animations, the clip and inline-target rules follow WCAG's own tests with self-tests, Actions are pinned to commit SHAs, and the generator pin moves to ssg 0.0.72.
