## Highlights ⭐️

* **Every theme passes SSG's quality gate**: The SSG credit link carries the `made-with-ssg` class the gate looks for, and the eight themes whose contact page had only a mailto link now have an accessible contact form that posts to `form_action`.
* **One header and footer per theme**: Atlas's tag pages use the theme's own stylesheet, toggle, header and footer, and their search works. French pages have French header buttons that stay in French, and home pages list their feeds again. A new build gate keeps every page of a theme consistent.
* **Voxt adds tsdev, jsdev and llamadev**: The suite table and footer list three more environments, and a focused link no longer sits under the two-row header at mid-widths.
