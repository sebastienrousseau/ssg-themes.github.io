## Highlights ⭐️

* **The diagnostic theme is passmcp**: Scout is renamed after the tool it showcases, with its layouts, gallery card and download names following. The gallery's `llms.txt` now describes it from its own manifest rather than with Curio's text, and lists Curio, jspassgen and the site theme it had left out.
* **sebastienrousseau.com ships as a theme**: The production theme of the research site joins the gallery as a site-tier theme. One shell replaces twelve 213 KB flat layouts, `tools/flatten.py` reproduces the site's `_layouts/` byte for byte, and the showcase build re-bases its root-absolute links onto `/sebastienrousseau/` so the demo navigates under its gallery path.
* **Site themes declare their own origins and budgets, and meet every gallery gate**: `theme.toml` gains `tier = "site"`, `allowed_origins`, `budget_kb` and `transfer_budget_kb`. A live site's own CDN, form and embed hosts count as first party, and its page weight and Lighthouse transfer are held to the budgets it declares, while every Lighthouse score threshold and every other gate applies unchanged. The site theme passes the three-state mode control, 44 px targets, 320 px reflow, AAA contrast measured from pixels, zoom and text spacing, print, forced colours, composition, focus, axe and pa11y suites, and Lighthouse scores 1.0 in every category on mobile because a first view paints in system faces and fetches the webfonts after load.
* **Control Centre and Cipher join the gallery**: Control Centre is an operations and security dashboard theme with a sidebar app shell, header search, a status ring joined by flows to the agents and servers either side, KPI tiles, severity pills and evidence tables. Its demo is a fictional company's MCP estate on example domains, in English and French, and its five-level severity palette is held to AAA in light and dark by the contrast gate. Cipher is a password and passphrase generator theme built from jspassgen's layouts, with its demo rewritten around a fictional offline generator in English and French; the full Diceware list ships packed inside `main.js`, and `make check-wordlist` now verifies both themes' lists.
