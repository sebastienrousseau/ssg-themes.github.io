## Highlights ⭐️

* **The diagnostic theme is passmcp**: Scout is renamed after the tool it showcases, with its layouts, gallery card and download names following.
* **sebastienrousseau.com ships as a theme**: The production theme of the research site joins the gallery as a site-tier theme. One shell replaces twelve 213 KB flat layouts, `tools/flatten.py` reproduces the site's `_layouts/` byte for byte, and the showcase build re-bases its root-absolute links onto `/sebastienrousseau/` so the demo navigates under its gallery path.
* **Site themes declare their own origins and budgets**: `theme.toml` gains `tier = "site"`, `allowed_origins`, `budget_kb` and `transfer_budget_kb`. A live site's own CDN, form and embed hosts count as first party, and its page weight and Lighthouse transfer are held to the budgets it declares, while every Lighthouse score threshold and every other gate applies unchanged.
* **The site theme meets every gallery gate**: the three-state mode control, 44 px targets, 320 px reflow, AAA contrast measured from pixels, zoom and text spacing, print, forced colours, composition, focus, axe and pa11y suites all pass, and Lighthouse scores 1.0 in every category on mobile because a first view paints in system faces and fetches the webfonts after load.
