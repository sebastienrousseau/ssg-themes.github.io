## Product first

The layout takes inspiration from monochrome fashion catalogues, but its jacket
silhouette and compositions are original CSS. It carries no copied brand,
photography or product design from the reference.

## Commerce without pressure

There are no artificial stock warnings, countdowns or preselected consent.
Product facts, care and service can be placed before a purchase decision.

## Strong in every scheme

Noir remains monochrome in spirit in light and dark modes while maintaining AAA
text contrast, visible focus and distinguishable controls.
