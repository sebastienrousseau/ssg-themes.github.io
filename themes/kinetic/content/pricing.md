## How this page works

The table above is an **island**: static HTML that a small module enhances
once it scrolls into view. Without JavaScript you get the complete monthly
table — which is also what a crawler indexes and what prints. The
billing-period control is created by the module, so the page never shows a
control that cannot work.

The table sits in its own horizontal scroll container, focusable from the
keyboard, so the columns stay reachable at 320px without the page itself
scrolling sideways.

## What the figures mean

Nothing. They are placeholders sized to look plausible in a layout. Replace
them with your own before deploying, along with the plan names.
