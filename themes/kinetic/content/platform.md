## Plan

Work is broken down until a unit fits inside a week. Dependencies are
declared once and drawn everywhere they matter, so a date that slips shows
up on the plan it affects rather than in somebody's inbox a fortnight later.

## Build

Boards, lists and a calendar are views over the same records. Changing the
view never changes the data, which is what lets two teams look at one
backlog through the lens each prefers without arguing about the tool.

## Review

Comments resolve against a revision rather than floating free. An approval
records who approved what, not merely that somebody did — the distinction
matters the first time a decision is questioned six months on.

## Report

Dashboards read the records the work already lives in. There is no export
step, so a number on a dashboard is never a fortnight behind the thing it
claims to describe.
