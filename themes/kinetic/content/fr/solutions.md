## Product teams

A roadmap that survives contact with a shipping date, because the roadmap
and the tickets are the same records. Moving a date moves the thing the date
was attached to.

## Marketing teams

A campaign calendar with the assets attached to the dates, so the question
"what is going out on Thursday" has one answer rather than three.

## Operations teams

Recurring work that does not depend on anyone remembering it, and a record
of what happened the last forty times it ran.

## Engineering teams

Incidents that leave a written trail without anyone writing a report. The
timeline is assembled from the record, not from memory.
