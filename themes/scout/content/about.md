## A shopping interface, not a dark pattern

Scout borrows the scale and modularity of modern agent products but avoids
countdowns, false scarcity and hidden sponsored ranking. The query card in the
hero is illustrative and does not pretend to be a working assistant.

## Make ranking legible

Real deployments should explain which factors affect order, how current the data
is, which retailers pay commission and how a person can reset personalisation.

## Designed for independent use

Every control has a visible label and large target. Content remains navigable by
keyboard, on touch, at zoom and without JavaScript.
