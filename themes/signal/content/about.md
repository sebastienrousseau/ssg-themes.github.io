## A dashboard-inspired system

Luminous blue surfaces, compact data cards and a restrained line graph evoke a
revenue workspace without shipping a heavy screenshot or application shell.
All visualisations are decorative; equivalent meaning remains in text.

## Explainability in the content model

The demonstration avoids absolute AI claims. It separates observed signals,
modelled scenarios and recommended actions so teams can explain what the
software knows and where judgement still belongs.

## Fast by construction

There are no external scripts, fonts, trackers or image requests. The static
output keeps interaction to navigation and colour preference only.
