## Designed for consequential information

Steward is built for organisations that need to communicate expertise without
turning trust into theatre. Its hierarchy pairs editorial serif display type
with compact operational labels, deliberate whitespace and a strict grid.

## Accessibility is structural

Every interactive target is at least 44 pixels in both dimensions. Content
reflows without horizontal scrolling at 320 pixels and at 400% zoom. Light,
dark, forced-colour, reduced-motion, print, no-JavaScript and no-CSS modes are
all treated as first-class presentations.

## Make the claims your own

The names, results and testimonials in the demonstration are fictional. Replace
them with evidence your organisation can publish, and keep qualifications close
to the figures they explain.
