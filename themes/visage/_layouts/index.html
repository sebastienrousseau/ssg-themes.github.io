Clear answers

Before you decide.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. Aesthetic consultation and medical diagnosis are different. Any clinical concern should be assessed through the appropriate healthcare pathway.

Who reviews my portrait?

A provider should identify the qualified professional responsible for review and clearly disclose any automated support.

What happens to my image?

Retention, access and deletion terms should be presented for consent before any file is submitted.

Do I have to proceed?

No. A consultation should leave room to pause, decline, request another opinion or take more time.
