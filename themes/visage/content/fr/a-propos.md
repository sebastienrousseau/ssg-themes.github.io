## A careful tone

Visage avoids before-and-after pressure, countdowns and claims of perfection.
Its language is deliberately neutral and its lavender accent is decoration,
never the only way information is communicated.

## Health content needs review

The theme is a design demonstration, not clinical or legal guidance. Any real
deployment should be reviewed by qualified practitioners and adapted to the
advertising, privacy and consent requirements of its jurisdiction.

## Inclusive by default

The reading measure, high-contrast palettes, keyboard behaviour and responsive
grid are tested across light, dark, forced-colour and reduced-motion settings.
