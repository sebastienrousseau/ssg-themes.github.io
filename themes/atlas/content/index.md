## What makes it a reading theme

The body column is set in a serif at a 72-character measure with looser
leading than the other themes in this suite. Navigation, buttons and table
headers stay on the interface face, so only the prose carries the serif —
a distinction that matters once a page runs past a screenful.

Corners are nearly square, shadows are faint, and there is no accent
gradient anywhere. The design gets out of the way.

## Structure over feed

A knowledge hub needs durable pages more than it needs a stream. Atlas
gives you an articles index, a papers index and an about page carrying
editorial standards, all built from Markdown you control.

RSS, Atom and JSON Feed are generated by the generator from the same
content and linked in the document head, so readers who do want a stream
get one without you maintaining a second thing.

## What it is not

Atlas ships no comment system, no newsletter capture, no share buttons and
no reading-progress bar. Each of those is a third-party request, a consent
obligation, or both. Add them deliberately if you need them.
