Cipher started in 2021 as a weekend tool for a family who kept losing their
router password. It now runs on desktops, in a terminal and in the browser,
and it still does one job: pick random words or characters and tell you how
many bits they are worth.

We charge teams for a licence and keep the personal edition free. There is no
cloud service to sell you, which is why there is nothing to sign in to.

## How we work

1. **Offline first**: Every build is tested with the network switched off. If a feature needs a server, we do not ship it.
2. **One published word list**: The app uses a 7,776-word Diceware list that we print in full, so anyone can check a result with five dice.
3. **Plain entropy**: The readout multiplies the number of picks by the bits per pick. We do not grade passwords with a mood colour or a guessed crack time.
4. **Readable everywhere**: The console works with a keyboard alone, at large text sizes, and in light or dark mode.
