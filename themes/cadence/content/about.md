## An original road composition

The rider, flare and wheel suggested in the hero are drawn entirely with CSS.
They create momentum without adding a blocking image request or making text
contrast depend on photography.

## Specifications need context

Real products should pair weight and performance figures with size, tolerance,
test method and conditions. The demonstration language models that discipline
instead of presenting unverifiable records.

## Made for every input

Navigation works with a keyboard, pointer, touch, zoom and no JavaScript. Motion
preferences are respected and essential text remains complete without styles.
