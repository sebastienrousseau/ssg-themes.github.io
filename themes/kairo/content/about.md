## A portfolio with range

Kairo takes its cue from cinematic art-direction portfolios: giant type,
cropped geometry and confident negative space. The composition is original and
uses no external photography, font service or tracking script.

## Drama without exclusion

Text always sits on a solid surface, never directly over decorative gradients.
The visual layer is hidden from assistive technology, while the information
hierarchy remains complete in HTML, print, forced colours and without CSS.

## Built to be changed

Replace the fictional projects with your own work, preserve meaningful heading
order, and give every image an informative alternative when you add media.
