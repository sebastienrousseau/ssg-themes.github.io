## What this theme is

Vista is the front page of a spatial-computing product that does not exist.
The specifications, the latencies and the pixel counts are illustrative:
they are here to exercise the components, not to describe a real device.

The category it belongs to — flagship hardware marketing — conventionally
opens with a full-bleed photograph and lays the headline across it. Vista
puts the headline above the photograph instead. Text on a photograph cannot
be contrast-checked, and a landscape whose brightness varies across its own
width is the worst possible ground for it.

## What is different about it

The feature rail scrolls, it does not play. Anything that moves for longer
than five seconds needs a way to stop it under WCAG 2.2.2, and a list of
six features has no reason to move on its own. It is an ordinary scroll
region with a name and a tab stop, so a trackpad, a drag, the arrow keys
and the two buttons all reach the same content. The buttons disable
themselves at each end rather than looking live and doing nothing.
