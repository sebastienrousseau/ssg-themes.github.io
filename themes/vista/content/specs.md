## Silicon

Two processors. One runs the system; the second does nothing but process
the sensor array, which is why the picture reaches the panels before the
inner ear registers a delay.

| Component | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Sensor-to-photon latency | 12 ms |
| Cameras and sensors | 17 |
| Microphones | 6 |
| Sustained refresh | 90–100 Hz |

## Optics

| Component | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Pixels, both panels | 23 million |
| Panel size | 27.5 mm diagonal |
| Colour | Wide gamut, 92% DCI-P3 |

## Materials and fit

An aluminium frame, a woven band and a light seal in three depths. The
weight sits on the cheekbones rather than the bridge of the nose, which is
the difference between forty minutes and four hours.

## What is not claimed

Nothing on this page describes a real product. The theme is the deliverable;
the device is a way to exercise it.
