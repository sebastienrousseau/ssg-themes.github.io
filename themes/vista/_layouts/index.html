A closer look

Built around what you actually do

01

An display for every task

Two panels the size of a postage stamp carry more pixels each than a 4K television, so text stays text at any distance.

02

Input without hardware

Look at a control and pinch. No wand, no glove, nothing to charge on a second cable overnight.

03

Sound that stays put

Audio is placed in the room rather than in your ears, so a window you moved to the left still sounds as though it is there.

04

Environments on demand

Replace the room with a landscape when you need to concentrate, and dial it back when somebody walks in.

05

Privacy at the sensor

Eye data never leaves the device and is never handed to an app. What you looked at is not a signal anybody else receives.

06

Passthrough that convinces

Cameras rebuild the room with low enough latency that reaching for a cup is not a calculation.

Silicon

Two chips, one of them doing nothing but listening

One runs the system. The second does nothing but process twelve cameras, five sensors and six microphones, so the picture is on the panels before your inner ear notices the delay.
