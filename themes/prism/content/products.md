## Core Ledger Engine

An immutable double-entry ledger that records millions of daily asset
movements with a cryptographic audit trail. Every posting is balanced at
write time, so reconciliation is a read rather than a nightly job, and a
balance is never a fortnight behind the entries that produced it.

## Global Treasury

Multi-currency reserve balances across 140 jurisdictions, with automated FX
hedging and inter-entity rebalancing. Liquidity moves to where it is needed
before the cut-off, not after the shortfall shows up in a report.

## Subscription Billing

Recurring billing for seat-based tiers, consumption meters and hybrid
contracts, with automated tax remittance and dunning that retries on a
schedule you can read. An invoice is generated from the same records the
usage was metered against, so the two cannot disagree.

## Marketplace Settlements

Payouts to tens of thousands of vendors and service providers, with
programmable split fees and local bank rails in each market. A platform
fee, a tax withholding and a vendor share are one instruction, settled
atomically or not at all.

## Risk & Heuristic Guard

Adaptive models score each transaction in under twenty milliseconds, with
every decision explained in terms an operations team can act on. A block is
a sentence, not a score.

## Static Frontend Architecture

Deterministic static delivery for the customer-facing surfaces: no layout
shift, subresource integrity on every asset, and a content security policy
that admits nothing from another origin. It is the architecture this theme
is built with, which is why the claim can be checked rather than asserted.
