## Multi-Tenant Software Platforms

Native payments, merchant accounts and embedded banking inside a software
product, with instant onboarding and a fee structure the platform sets
itself. The platform's customers never leave its interface, and the
platform never touches a card number.

## Sustainable Asset Management

Carbon-audited settlement rails and automated governance reporting, tracked
against the general ledger rather than in a spreadsheet beside it. An
impact figure is derived from the postings it describes, so it is as
current as the ledger is.

## Institutional Wealth Custody

Digital custody for private banks, family offices and endowments:
multi-signatory authorisation, hardware-isolated key material and
line-level audit controls. Every action names who approved it and under
which mandate.

## Global Direct-to-Consumer Commerce

Localised checkout in 140 markets, with native currencies, dynamic tax
calculation and local payment methods. Cross-border abandonment falls when
a customer pays the way they already pay, in the currency they already
think in.
