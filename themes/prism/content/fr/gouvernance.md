## Security & Encryption

Key material is generated and held in hardware security modules, data is
encrypted at rest and in transit, and every administrative action is
recorded against a named person and a mandate. The controls are attested
annually under SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001; the attestation letters are
available to customers under NDA.

## Sustainability Accounting

Every transaction can carry a carbon-offset posting alongside its financial
one, derived from the same ledger entry. Sustainability figures are
therefore reconciled the way money is, and are exactly as current.

## Accessibility Conformance

The customer-facing surfaces target WCAG 2.2 at level AAA for colour
contrast, target size and focus visibility, and the claim is checked by
automated gates on every change. This theme is one of those surfaces: the
gates it passes are listed in the repository it ships from.

## About the Company

Prism is a fictional company that exists to give this theme something to
be about. Nothing on these pages describes a real product, customer,
figure or attestation.
