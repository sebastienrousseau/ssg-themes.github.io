## An image-free furniture study

The upholstered form in the hero is drawn with CSS, inspired by the quiet scale
of contemporary catalogue photography without reproducing a particular product.
Real product imagery can be added with intrinsic dimensions and descriptive
alternatives.

## Information before conversion

Dimensions, materials, access requirements, delivery, returns and care belong
close to the product. The theme avoids hiding them behind interaction or making
the purchase control the only obvious route.

## Comfortable at every viewport

Layouts collapse into a clear single reading order on small screens and remain
bounded on wide displays. Text spacing, focus and contrast survive zoom and user
preferences.
