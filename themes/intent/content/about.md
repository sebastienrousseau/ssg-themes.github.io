## Designed as an editorial object

The serif scale, black-and-white field and yellow studio ribbon echo independent
design publishing while remaining an original, responsive composition.

## Case studies with substance

Beautiful images are not enough. Each project section is structured to explain
the problem, design choice and result, with credits and roles close to the work.

## Expression that remains usable

Decorative layers never carry meaning. Reading order, controls and contrast are
stable in forced colours, print, no-CSS and zoomed presentations.
